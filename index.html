Saved on this device only.

    Speak: tap a mic, talk, then tap it again. The translation appears on the other side and is read aloud.

    On iPhone: tap the text box, then the mic key on the keyboard. Dictation follows the keyboard's language, so add Korean and Spanish keyboards in Settings › General › Keyboard and tap the globe key to switch before each person speaks.

    Better iPhone voices: Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices. Download the Premium or Enhanced voice for Korean, English and Spanish (Mexico). The app picks the best one it finds.

    Face to face: lay the phone flat between you. The top half's words turn to face the person across the table, so they look upside down to you. That is on purpose. Use "Swap who's on top" to choose which language faces them.

    Formality: on 자동 Auto, the app listens to how the Korean speaker talks. 존댓말 in means 존댓말 back, 반말 in means 반말 back. Spanish follows the same rule: 존댓말 becomes usted, 반말 becomes tú.

    Scenes: English shows Canada, 한국어 shows Korea, Español shows Mexico. The side that is speaking comes forward, then the translation's country takes over.

    Run the formality eval to check how well Puente matches 존댓말, 반말, usted and tú.
